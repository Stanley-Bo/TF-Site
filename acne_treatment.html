How It Works

9 steps. One clear result.

A structured professional treatment — every step targets a different layer of the acne cycle.

01

Cleanse

A thorough double cleanse removes makeup, sunscreen, excess sebum and surface impurities — creating a clean slate before treatment begins.

02

Gentle Scrub

A mild exfoliant removes dead skin cells and loosens congestion in the pores, making extraction smoother and more effective without irritating breakout-prone skin.

03

Steam Therapy

Warm steam softens the skin and dilates pores — making blocked sebum and impurities easier to remove cleanly and with minimal discomfort.

04

Extraction

Professional manual extraction of blackheads, whiteheads and congested pores. Performed precisely to clear blockages without damaging surrounding skin.

05

High Frequency

A high-frequency device delivers a gentle electrical current to the skin — killing acne-causing bacteria, reducing inflammation and accelerating the healing of post-extraction skin.

06

Soothing Gel & Cooling Hammer

Calming gel is applied and worked in with a cold cooling hammer — reducing post-extraction redness, tightening pores and bringing immediate relief to sensitised skin.

07

Detox Point Massage

Targeted lymphatic drainage massage around key facial points encourages the removal of toxins and reduces puffiness, supporting the skin's natural healing response.

08

Soothing Mask

A calming treatment mask is applied to reduce residual redness, hydrate and restore the skin barrier — leaving skin settled, nourished and noticeably clearer.

09

Post Treatment Care

A lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturiser and SPF finish the session — sealing in the treatment, protecting the freshly cleared skin and prepping it for the hours ahead.

FAQ

Good to know before you come in

Answers to what our clients ask most often.

Some discomfort during extraction is normal, especially over congested areas. The steam therapy beforehand softens pores significantly, and our therapists work carefully to minimise discomfort. The cooling hammer step immediately after helps calm the skin down.

Some post-treatment redness is expected, particularly over extraction areas. This typically subsides within a few hours. We recommend booking when you have some downtime — ideally in the evening or before a rest day.

For active acne, once every 3–4 weeks is generally recommended — this aligns with your skin's natural cell turnover cycle. As your skin improves, you can space sessions out to monthly maintenance. Our therapist will advise based on your skin's response.

Please let us know if your skin is particularly sensitive or reactive before booking. We can adjust the pressure and technique — for example, skipping the scrub or reducing extraction intensity — to suit your skin's tolerance.

High frequency uses a safe, low-level electrical current passed through a glass electrode to produce ozone, which kills surface bacteria and reduces inflammation. It's a well-established, non-invasive treatment used widely in professional skincare. If you have a pacemaker or epilepsy, please let us know before booking.

Keep it simple — avoid heavy makeup for the rest of the day, stay out of direct sun, and don't touch or pick at the skin. Use a gentle cleanser and apply SPF the next morning. We'll walk you through the full aftercare at the end of your session.
